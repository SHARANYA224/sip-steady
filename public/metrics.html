4. Experiment sample size

Two-sided test, 95% confidence, 80% power: n per arm = (1.96·√(2p̄(1−p̄)) + 0.84·√(p₁(1−p₁)+p₂(1−p₂)))² ÷ (p₂−p₁)²
What we want to detectBaselineNeeded per arm
5-point lift in Soft-Choice Rate30%~1,380
5-point lift (worst case)50%~1,570
2-point lift in month-12 active rate61%~9,250

The deck plans ~10,000 cancel intents per arm. That is well above the ~1,600 needed for the 5-point lift, which leaves room to read results by segment (salaried vs stipend, cash vs returns trigger) and to detect a change as small as 2 points in the month-12 North Star.
