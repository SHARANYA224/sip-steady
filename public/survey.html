Insights

  • The leak is early: 26 of 46 (57%) first paused or stopped within 3 months; only 4 never did.
  • Two triggers dominate: money tight (22, 48%) and returns lower than expected (20, 43%).
  • Expectation gap: 20 (43%) expected steady growth every month.
  • Understanding gap: 25 of 45 (56%) rate 1–2 on "I understand whether my SIP is on track".
  • Help comes from outside the app: YouTube 23 (50%), friends/family 17 (37%), the app 10 (22%).
