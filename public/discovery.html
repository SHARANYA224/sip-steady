What the reviews say

  • Easy to start: 287 reviews praise how simple it is to begin (avg 4.8★). Starting is not the problem.
  • Trouble comes after: support (109), redemption (68), confusing portfolio numbers (55), trust in charges (43), autopay failures (29).
  • Control gap: 17 reviews ask to skip, pause or change a SIP; one user recovering from surgery couldn't skip a month.
  • 2026 spike: 86 of 287 SIP reviews this year are 1–2★ (30%), vs 10% in 2024–25.

What the survey says

  • 57% first paused or stopped within 3 months.
  • Money tight (48%) and returns lower than expected (43%) are the top reasons.
  • 56% can't tell if their SIP is on track; 43% expected steady growth every month.
  • When unsure: YouTube 50%, friends 37%, the app 22%.

Explain the stop moment

Softer choices than cancel

Reviews: 17 ask to skip, pause or change the amount; skip works only up to 2 days before. Build: skip, pause or lower offered inside the same flow.

Recover failed instalments

Reviews: 29 autopay or mandate failures, no retry. Survey: 39% have a SIP date away from income. Build: move the date after salary; remind the day before.

  3. Label: keyword taxonomy of 9 themes, multi-label. 487 reviews matched at least one theme; 204 were generic praise or off-topic. Gemini re-labelling with the SIP Steady key is the next step, checked against a 20-review hand-labelled golden set.
  4. Cluster: themes ranked by count, share of 1–2★, and average rating; trend by year.

Limits: Play Store reviews skew to extremes and to app-wide issues; keyword labels miss sarcasm and Hinglish; the survey is a convenience sample.
