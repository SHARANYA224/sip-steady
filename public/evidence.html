5. Corrections made

  1. Removed synthetic data. 56 test rows submitted to check the survey pipeline were all deleted before any analysis, so no test data reaches the findings.
  2. Excluded inconsistent answers. 21 SIP responses removed by stated rules: pause date later than SIP age (Q3 vs Q10), "never paused" with status paused/stopped (Q9 vs Q10), "not earning" with "own income" (Q21 vs Q22), and repeated contact numbers.
  3. Updated retired AI models (after submission). Google retired Gemini 2.5 Flash for new keys; the app now tries current Flash models in order and still falls back to vetted answers if all fail.
  4. Fixed incomplete answers. 11 key questions were made required mid-collection after early answers came in incomplete; Q17 scale labels were clarified.

6. Key findings (n = 46 clean)

  • 57% first paused or stopped within 3 months; 91% have paused, reduced or stopped at least once.
  • Top reasons: money tight (48%), returns lower than expected (43%), forgot (24%).
  • 43% expected steady growth every month; 56% can't tell if their SIP is on track.
  • When unsure: YouTube 50%, friends 37%, the app 22%.
  • Play Store: 287 reviews say starting is easy; problems after start: support (109), redemption (68), confusing numbers (55), autopay failures (29), can't skip/pause (17).

10. Gemini review labelling (added 9 Nov 2026, after submission)

The discovery engine's keyword labels were re-checked with Google Gemini (/label). Gemini labelled the same 20 hand-labelled Play Store reviews.

MeasureResult
Gemini shares at least one theme with the human label19 / 20 (95%)
Exact match of all themes17 / 20 (85%)
Disagreements"great app for sip" (human: easy to start; Gemini: no theme); two multi-theme reviews where Gemini picked a subset

Gemini then labelled 165 of 326 SIP reviews collected live (4,800 reviews in total) before the free-tier daily quota ran out. In those 165: easy to start 46, support 42, confusing portfolio numbers 26, trust/charges 20, autopay failures 18, redemption 14, guidance 14, can't skip/pause 7. The order of the main problem themes matches the keyword engine.
